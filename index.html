Nester Engine Publisher

Nester Engine Publisher is a private content management and publishing tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It uploads videos the owner has produced to the owner's own channels on YouTube through the official YouTube Data API, and sets each video's title, description, tags, thumbnail and scheduled publish time.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
